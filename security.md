## 🌴 Your Mii Island, Now On PC

*Tomodachi Life* is one of the most unique and beloved life simulation games ever released by Nintendo. It allows you to create Mii characters, watch them form relationships, get into bizarre arguments, and live out hilariously unpredictable lives on a tropical island. However, the game was strictly locked to the Nintendo 3DS family of consoles. Until now.

**Nintendo Tomodachi Life On PC** is a comprehensive, community-driven guide and toolkit designed to help you emulate this beloved classic on your modern computer. This repository doesn't just give you the emulator; it provides a meticulously curated setup, including high-definition texture packs, 60 FPS patches, and custom save files to get you started instantly.

Whether you are a nostalgic fan who wants to relive the chaos of your old island or a newcomer eager to see what all the fuss is about, this repository is your ultimate destination. We provide detailed, step-by-step instructions for setting up the Citra emulator, configuring your controls, and applying the best visual mods. The goal is to make the game look and feel better than it ever did on the original hardware.

## 🎒 What's Inside The Toolkit?

Instead of complex command-line scripts, we have packaged everything you need into a simple, user-friendly format. This repository is designed to be as plug-and-play as possible.

### 🗃️ The Mods & Textures Folder
Inside the `assets/` directory, you will find a meticulously organized collection of HD textures, custom UI elements, and game mods. These files are pre-configured to seamlessly drop into your Citra emulator's load directory. No manual file renaming is required.

### 🎮 The Controller Profiles
We have included ready-to-use input profiles. Whether you prefer the layout of an Xbox controller or the classic feel of a PlayStation DualSense, simply import the profile into Citra, and your buttons will be mapped perfectly for the game's unique touch-screen interactions.

Our GitHub Wiki is the true heart of this project. It features an incredibly detailed, step-by-step guide covering everything from installing the Citra emulator to configuring your graphics settings. ### 💾 Pre-built Save Files
Want to skip the tutorial and dive straight into the drama? We have included community-shared save files featuring fully populated islands with dozens of unique Mii characters. Just load the save file, and you are instantly part of the community.

## 🧠 How The Emulation Works

Emulating the Nintendo 3DS is a complex feat of software engineering. The 3DS uses a dual-core ARM11 CPU and a custom PICA200 GPU. Citra, the emulator used in this guide, translates these instructions to x86-64 in real-time.

What makes *Tomodachi Life* particularly tricky to emulate is its heavy reliance on the 3DS's dual-screen setup and its real-time clock. Our setup guides automatically configure Citra to display both screens side-by-side or stacked vertically, depending on your preference. We also include patches that allow the game to sync with your PC's system clock, ensuring that Mii events happen at the correct time.

The HD texture pack is another highlight. The original 3DS had a resolution of just 400x240. Our custom textures are rendered at 4K, meaning every blade of grass, every piece of clothing, and every facial expression on your Mii is incredibly sharp and detailed. Combined with the 60 FPS patch, the game feels like a modern remaster.

The *Tomodachi Life* community is one of the most creative and dedicated in gaming. If you have designed a custom texture pack, found a new performance tweak, or want to share your hilarious Mii moments, we welcome your contributions!

1. **Fork** the repository.
2. **Create** your feature branch (`git checkout -b feature/new-texture`).
3. **Commit** your changes (`git commit -m 'Add new texture'`).
4. **Push** to the branch (`git push origin feature/new-texture`).
5. **Open** a Pull Request.

Please read our `CONTRIBUTING.md` for detailed guidelines on submitting texture packs and performance patches. We also have an active Discord server where you can share your island's drama, ask for help with emulator settings, and discuss the latest news about a potential sequel.
